# Operating Charter — Soul In PsyAbstract LLC

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-06-27

---

## Mission

Build AI infrastructure that empowers neurodivergent creators — ADHD, BPD, autism spectrum — to create, ship, and thrive without the cognitive overhead that neurotypical systems assume.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Neurodivergence-first** — all products are designed for neurodivergent brains, not adapted from neurotypical defaults.

2. **Transparency** — public governance, open tokenomics, verifiable on-chain data.

3. **Creator sovereignty** — artists and builders own their work and data.

4. **Forensic integrity** — every canonical system change has a hash, timestamp, and audit trail.

5. **No UI overhead** — SIPA Protocol Zero: prefer API/CLI over reload-heavy UIs wherever technically sound.
