## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship (the "Purpose") and may disclose confidential information to each other in connection with this Purpose.

---

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party, including but not limited to:

- Business plans, strategies, and financial projections
- Technical architecture, source code, and AI system design (including SIPA OS, MLL layers, SIPA Protocol Zero)
- Product roadmaps and unreleased features
- Customer and user data
- API keys, credentials, and security protocols
- Proprietary algorithms and ML models
- Investor terms and fundraising details
- Art collection details, pricing, and unreleased works

---

## 3. Obligations

Each party agrees to:

(a) Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence  
(b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent  
(c) Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose  
(d) Limit access to employees or contractors with a need to know, bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations  
(e) Notify the disclosing party promptly upon discovering any unauthorized disclosure

---

## 4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement  
(b) Was already known to the Recipient before disclosure  
(c) Is independently developed by the Recipient without use of Confidential Information  
(d) Is required to be disclosed by law or court order (with prompt written notice to the disclosing party)

---

## 5. Intellectual Property

Nothing in this Agreement grants any license or rights to any intellectual property. All Confidential Information remains the sole property of the disclosing party.

SIPA OS architecture, MLL framework, SIPA Crisis Rail, and all related systems remain proprietary to Soul In PsyAbstract LLC under the SIPA CORE CANON PROTOCOL.

---

## 6. Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect for **two (2) years** from the date of signing, unless terminated earlier by mutual written consent. Obligations regarding trade secrets survive indefinitely.

---

## 7. Return of Information

Upon request or termination, each party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information of the other party and certify such destruction in writing.

---

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of **Delaware**, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Delaware.

---

## 9. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes all prior discussions on this subject.
