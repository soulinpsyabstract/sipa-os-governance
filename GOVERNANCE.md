# Governance Structure — Soul In PsyAbstract LLC

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-06-27  
**Status:** Active

---

## Decision-Making Model

Soul In PsyAbstract LLC operates as a **Founder-Led LLC** with defined roles and graduated voting authority.

### Roles

| Role | Holder | Authority |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Founder / Chief Architect | Marina (Aelin) Davidov | System architecture, product direction, brand identity, final decisions |
| Co-Founder / Operations | Marina (Aelin) Davidov | Day-to-day operations, deployments, financial management |
| Advisor / Equity Holder | Natasha Jigalov | Advisory input, 5% equity stake |

### Decision Tiers

**Tier 1 — Operational (no approval needed):**
- Code deployments and product updates
- Marketing content and campaigns
- Vendor selection under $500/month
- Hiring freelancers and contractors

**Tier 2 — Significant (Founder decision required):**
- New product launches or major pivots
- Equity grants or SAFEs
- Vendor contracts over $500/month
- Fundraising terms and investor agreements
- IP licensing

**Tier 3 — Constitutional (requires written amendment):**
- Changes to LLC Operating Agreement
- New full member admissions
- Token governance changes affecting supply or allocation
- Dissolution or merger

## Transparency Commitments

1. **Public governance documents** — this repository is the public record.
2. **Open source components** — SIPA OS infrastructure released under MIT where feasible.
3. **Token transparency** — all SIPA token allocations, lockups, and LP positions are publicly verifiable on Base mainnet.
4. **IP protection** — all proprietary systems are declared under SIPA CORE CANON PROTOCOL.

---

## Conflict Resolution

Disputes between members are resolved in order:
1. Good-faith negotiation (7 days)
2. Mediation via mutually agreed third party (14 days)
3. Binding arbitration under Delaware law

---

## Governance Updates

All amendments to this document require:
- Written proposal by any member
- 7-day comment period
- Founder approval
- Entry in [AMENDMENTS.md](./AMENDMENTS.md) with date and hash
