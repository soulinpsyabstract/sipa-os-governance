## 1. Who We Are

Soul In PsyAbstract LLC ("SIPA", "we", "us") operates SIPA OS — an AI infrastructure platform — and the Soul In PsyAbstract art brand. Our products include SIPA Focus, SIPA NeuroPower, SIPA AI, SIPA Shell, and soulinpsyabstract.store.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Account data | Email, name | Authentication (Auth0) |
| Usage data | Feature interactions, session data | Product improvement, analytics (PostHog) |
| Payment data | Transaction references | Payment processing (NowPayments, Wise, PayPal, Payoneer) |
| AI session data | Chat messages, coaching sessions | Delivering AI responses (Nebius, DeepSeek) |
| Art purchase data | Shipping address, order details | Order fulfillment |

We do **not** sell personal data to third parties.

---

## 3. How We Use Data

- Provide and improve our services
- Process payments and orders
- Send transactional emails (order confirmations, account updates)
- Safety monitoring (SIPA Crisis Rail — for mental health AI features)
- Legal compliance

---

## 4. AI & Mental Health Features

SIPA AI and SIPA NeuroCoach process conversation data to deliver coaching responses. For safety:
- Crisis detection keywords trigger immediate safety responses
- Session data is not used to train third-party models
- All AI inference runs through Nebius Serverless AI (GDPR-aware)
